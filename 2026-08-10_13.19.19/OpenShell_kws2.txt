By default, the key-encryption key is created at `$XDG_STATE_HOME/openshell/gateway/credentials/key-encryption-key.bin` with owner-only permissions. Use `[openshell.gateway.credential_storage] key_encryption_key_env` instead of `key_encryption_key_path` to load a base64-encoded 32-byte key-encryption key from an environment variable.
